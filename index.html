Our Core Mandate

Transforming Fragmented Industrial Salvage into Governed Cashflows

Industrial businesses generate valuable secondary materials, production off-cuts and recoverable inventory. Yet identifying the right buyers, verifying material quality, coordinating logistics and reconciling transactions can involve fragmented processes and multiple intermediaries.

IND-VIA aims to connect these activities through a managed, technology-enabled commercial network. We believe industrial commerce can become more transparent, traceable and efficient when material discovery, physical execution and commercial documentation work together.

Our Operating Focus

Building an integrated, asset-light network that helps industrial businesses recover value from secondary materials while improving coordination across the transaction lifecycle.

Material Discovery

Connecting verified industrial material generators with qualified commercial end-buyers directly, eliminating multi-layered broker cartels.

Quality Verification

Supporting pre-dispatch inspection, spectrographic assays, and certified material documentation to prevent plant gate rejections.

Logistics Coordination

Connecting material movement with operational milestones, weighbridge integrations, and digital e-way bill generation.

Reconciliation & Assurance

Linking purchase orders, delivery records, quality documentation, GST compliance (Section 51 TDS & RCM), and automated escrow disbursal.
